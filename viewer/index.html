Loss

Most "declared loss" isn't real

Genuine wire loss (qlog-confirmed, never later covered by a packets_acked) sits flat near 1–3% and doesn't move with the threshold, as expected of a network property. The double-digit "declared" rate the detector reports is almost entirely spurious reordering.

Multipath

Path selection isn't RTT-aware

The scheduler is a no-op: traffic just always prefers the lowest PathId. Mesh (0% configured loss, 2ms delay) usually wins that race by finishing its handshake first — not by being chosen for its RTT.
